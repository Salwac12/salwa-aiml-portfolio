AI-Based Visual Defect Detection System

A planned computer-vision inspection app that identifies unusual-looking products and shows a heatmap of suspected defects.

Problem to solve

Manual product inspection can be slow and inconsistent. The proposed application would learn the normal appearance of one product type, then evaluate new images for visible anomalies.

Proposed workflow

  • Begin with normal images from one category in MVTec AD, such as bottles.
  • Train or configure PatchCore through Anomalib to model normal appearance.
  • Allow users to upload a product photo for analysis.
  • Show a normal/defective prediction, anomaly score and heatmap for suspicious regions.
  • Explore inspection history and downloadable inspection reports.

Planned technologies

Python · Anomalib · PatchCore · PyTorch · Streamlit.

Dataset plan

Start with a single MVTec AD category and later test photographs of personally owned objects. The dataset's applicable usage terms will be checked before use or redistribution.

My planned contribution

Design and build the Streamlit interface, integrate the Anomalib inference workflow, add inspection history and report downloads, test sample images and document observations.

MailShield AI / SecureMailScope

AI-assisted cryptographic security posture assessment for email communications.

The challenge

Email conversations can be affected by outdated TLS versions, weak cipher suites, invalid certificates and unsafe STARTTLS behavior. Manually interpreting large PCAP captures makes these risks difficult to prioritize.

Proposed workflow

  • Read PCAP/PCAPNG captures and reconstruct SMTP, IMAP, POP3 sessions.
  • Review encryption negotiations, TLS handshakes and X.509 certificates.
  • Combine security rules with anomaly detection to assess risks.
  • Generate a 0–100 posture score, prioritized findings and reports.

Proposed technology stack

Python, TShark, Scapy, OpenSSL, cryptography, Scikit-learn (Isolation Forest), FastAPI, React, Tailwind, PostgreSQL/SQLite, ReportLab and Docker.

Evidence & integrity angle

Related project notes propose hashing PCAP files, reports and approved findings for a tamper-evident audit trail. Sensitive message contents and full certificates are not intended to be stored on-chain.

Where AI fits

Anomaly detection can flag unusual TLS-handshake patterns alongside deterministic security rules; analyst review remains essential.

SIH 2026Team VerdeX · Concept

EcoTrust AI

AI-powered sustainability verification to help identify greenwashing.

The challenge

Sustainability claims can be difficult to substantiate when supplier documents, certifications and environmental data are scattered or incomplete.

Proposed workflow

  • Collect supplier, product and certification documents.
  • Validate information and compare claims against independent evidence.
  • Use AI to identify gaps and inconsistencies, with expert verification.
  • Generate a sustainability score, ESG insights, a verified badge and a QR profile.

Proposed technology stack

React.js, HTML/CSS/JavaScript, Python (FastAPI/Flask), TensorFlow, Scikit-learn, spaCy, Google Vision/Tesseract OCR, PostgreSQL, cloud storage and Chart.js.

What makes it interesting

This concept connects document understanding with evidence-based decision-making. It is designed to help people evaluate environmental claims rather than accept marketing statements at face value.

Scope

The shared material is a project proposal and describes planned product features; no performance metrics or live verification service are claimed.

SIH 2026 · SIH26151Team VerdeX · Concept

Dark Web Threat Actor Research

Exploring correlation of public clues for cyber-threat intelligence.

The challenge

Online threat actors may use multiple aliases, infrastructures and cryptocurrency identifiers. Researchers need methods for assessing links among disparate signals without treating uncertain correlations as proof of identity.

Proposed workflow

  • Analyze authorized, publicly available infrastructure clues and metadata.
  • Correlate handles, public PGP keys and cryptocurrency wallet identifiers in an identity graph.
  • Evaluate writing-style similarities as one additional signal.
  • Combine clues into confidence assessments and export a reviewable report.

Proposed technology stack

Python, TLS/SSL and favicon-hash correlation, graph analytics, Jaro–Winkler matching, PyTorch/DeBERTa stylometry, Bayesian confidence scoring, Neo4j and Elasticsearch.

Responsible interpretation

Proposed attribution methods are research hypotheses; writing style, shared infrastructure or wallet links do not independently establish a person's identity. Human review, privacy and legal authorization are essential.

Project status

Concept study based on two versions of the same SIH 2026 presentation, not two separate projects.
